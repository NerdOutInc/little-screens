02 What you’ll need

  • A TRMNL-compatible device

    Wi-Fi and firmware that lets you use a custom server.

  • An always-on computer

    Keep it awake, connected to the internet, and running Codex desktop for regular updates.

  • Codex desktop + the Sites plugin

    An account with access to both. Codex creates your Site and updates its images from your computer.

Codex can help install Node.js 22.13+ and Python 3.10+, the setup tools. Google Calendar is optional.

Tested hardware & compatibility

Our local prototype was tested on TRMNL OG, firmware 1.8.17. The current renderer produces 800 × 480 images. Other devices may need adjustments; other models and pairing with the hosted Site are not yet verified.
